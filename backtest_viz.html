Summary — What Changed in v2
  • Regime gating is the single biggest discovery. HV20 below 35% = signals are statistical losers. Scanner now warns on [calm] setups.
  • Inside day is inverted — it works in calm stocks (Sharpe 5.44), not volatile ones. Scanner weights accordingly.
  • Gap-up = fade. Not continuation. Gap-up + HV = puts, not calls (Sharpe 1.76 on the fade, -0.32 on continuation).
  • Hold period matters. Overnight is peak. 3D hold turns highvol from Sharpe 2.48 → -0.20. Don't hold past day 1.
  • HV+GD is the best single setup: gap fills on volatile stocks, Sharpe 3.10, WR 55%.
  • Options sim is a floor estimate — real intraday entries outperform EOD model. Stock direction signal is the reliable metric.
