FLOW.SCANNER
0DTE options flow scanner — reads the market, finds the setups, recommends the contract
What This Does
FlowScanner scans a universe of stocks and ETFs every time you hit ▶ SCAN. It reads price action, volume, volatility, and sector momentum — then surfaces tickers that have a tradeable setup for that day. Enable Contracts and it also picks the best options contract (strike, expiry, delta) for each signal. Think of it as your morning prep tool: run it before the open, see what's setting up, plan your trade.
How To Use It
1. Hit ▶ SCAN
Leave the ticker box blank to scan the full default universe (~55 tickers). Or type specific tickers like NVDA AMD TSLA.
2. Enable Contracts
Check the Contracts box before scanning. It fetches live options chains and recommends a specific strike + expiry for each signal.
3. Read the table
Grade A/B tickers are the strongest setups. Click any row to expand it — you'll see the contract detail, key levels, and signal breakdown.
4. Check sector heat
The colored tiles at the top show which sectors are moving. Trade with the hot sectors — avoid fading them early in the day.
Column Reference
Grade (A / B / C / D)
Overall signal quality. A = all systems go. B = solid setup, minor caveat. C = weak. D = skip it.
Setup (badges)
The pattern detected — see Signals section below for what each one means.
Dir (↑ CALLS / ↓ PUTS)
Which direction the setup favors. Calls = bullish play. Puts = bearish play.
RelVol
Relative volume vs the 10-day average. 2x+ = unusually active, confirms the move. Under 1x = low conviction.
HV20 + Regime
20-day historical volatility. Volatile (≥35%) = options setups have best edge. Normal (20–35%) = standard. Calm (<20%) = skip vol plays.
RS (Relative Strength)
How the stock is performing vs SPY today. +2%+ = outperforming. Negative = lagging the market.
OScore (0–100)
Options attractiveness score. Combines RelVol, HV regime, and delta. 75+ = strong. 50–74 = decent. Under 50 = weak.
IVR (IV Rank proxy)
Where implied volatility sits relative to its recent range. High IVR (70+) = IV is expensive, favor selling. Low IVR (≤30) = IV is cheap, favor buying.
EM± (Expected Move)
The 1-sigma expected daily price move based on HV20. Example: ±2.1% means the market expects a ~2% range today.
Contract
The recommended options contract — strike, expiry, and mid-price. Only shows when Contracts is checked. Click the row to see delta, IV, and ROI.
Signal Badges Explained
HV★ (HV Pure) — green bright
Best setup. Stock is in a volatile HV regime with no conflicting pattern. Backtested Sharpe ~4.9. High conviction directional play.
HV+ID — green
High vol + Inside Day. The stock coiled inside yesterday's range while volatile. Sharpe ~3.1. Wait for the breakout of today's range.
HV+G↓ — green
High vol + Gap Down. Stock gapped down — bias is for a fill back toward prior close. Sharpe ~2.9. Play calls if gap fill confirmed.
HV+G↑FADE — red bright
High vol + Gap Up — fade it. Backtests show gap-ups in volatile regimes tend to fail. Sharpe ~2.4 on the put side.
ID (Inside Day) — purple
Price compressed inside yesterday's range. Wait — don't trade until it breaks above the high or below the low. Then trade the breakout.
BK (Breakout) — cyan
Price broke above a key level with volume confirmation. Don't fade it — trade in the direction of the break.
G↑ / G↓ (Gap) — yellow/cyan
Stock opened with a gap from prior close. G↓ gaps tend to fill (bullish). G↑ gaps tend to stall or fade in volatile regimes.
LAG+X% — cyan
Sector laggard. The sector is running but this stock hasn't moved yet. Play the catch-up — it tends to close the gap within the session.
⚠CALM — dim
Warning: HV-based signals lose edge when the stock is in a calm volatility regime. Reduce size or skip.
★LVL / ★★LVL — red
Price is near a key support or resistance level. Strong levels (★★) tend to cause sharp reactions — either a bounce or a break.
Sector Heat Map
The colored tiles show all 10 S&P sectors sorted hottest to coldest left to right. Green = sector gaining, Red = sector losing. Intensity = magnitude. Use it to know the market's narrative before you trade: if Technology is bright green and Energy is red, trade with tech momentum. The ETF ticker (XLK, XLF, etc.) is shown under each name so you can pull up the chart yourself.
Filters & Sorting
Any Setup
Shows everything with a signal. Good starting point for a full market read.
Grade A Only
Only the highest-conviction setups. Use this when you want one clean trade instead of a list.
Sector Laggards
Stocks whose sector is ripping but the stock itself hasn't moved. Classic catch-up trade.
Dynamic mode
Adds today's top movers to the scan universe. Good for catching breakouts outside the default list.
