How To Use
1
Check the sector heat — green sectors are moving. Trade with them, not against.
2

3

What The Columns Mean
Grade A→DSignal quality. A = strong, D = skip.
Dir↑ CALLS = buy calls (bullish) · ↓ PUTS = buy puts (bearish)
RelVolVolume vs normal. 2x+ = real move. Under 1x = low conviction.
HV20Volatility level. VOLATILE = best edge · NORMAL = ok · CALM = skip vol plays
RSStock vs SPY today. +2%+ = outperforming · negative = lagging
OScoreOptions score 0–100. 75+ = strong · 50–74 = ok · under 50 = weak
IVRIs IV cheap or expensive? 70+ = expensive · 30 or less = cheap, good to buy
EM±Expected daily move. ±2% means the market expects a ~2% range today.
ContractRecommended options contract. Click the row to see delta, IV, strike, price.
Signal Badges
HV★Best setup. High volatility, clean signal. High conviction play.
HV+IDHigh vol + price coiled inside yesterday's range. Wait for the breakout, then trade it.
HV+G↓High vol + gapped down. Bias = calls, price tends to fill the gap back up.
HV+G↑FADEHigh vol + gapped up. Fade it — puts. Gap-ups in volatile stocks tend to reverse.
IDInside Day. Price is coiling. Don't trade yet — wait for it to break above/below yesterday's range.
BKBreakout with volume. Trade with it, don't fade.
LAG+X%Sector is running, this stock hasn't moved yet. Catch-up play.
G↑Gapped up. Continuation lean, but cautious — can stall.
G↓Gapped down. Fill bias — stock tends to recover toward prior close.
⚠CALMWarning: low volatility environment. Vol-based signals have no edge here. Skip or size down.
